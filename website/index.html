Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers before you launch.

Does MarketOS post or spend ad budget automatically?

No. Every AI-generated post, email, or ad draft starts in Draft or Pending Approval. Nothing enters your scheduled queue or launches paid ads without explicit owner approval.

Can I manage businesses in different countries under one account?

Yes. Each workspace maintains its own country, adapted postal/state format, billing currency, display currency, language, and time zone.

Will the web platform connect with the MarketOS Android app?

Yes—both share the same MarketOS data model, workspace structure, and approval-gated workflow so teams can move seamlessly between web and mobile.
